Car wash & detailing · Suomenoja, Espoo

A proper hand wash at Hannuksenpelto, five minutes from Länsiväylä.

Happy wash sits in the Hannuksenpelto business cluster near Suomenoja and Friisinmäki, easy to reach from Länsiväylä whether you are coming from Helsinki or Kirkkonummi. Every car is washed by hand, booked by phone, and finished while you wait.

Wash & detailing menu

Prices are starting prices for a standard passenger car. Vans, SUVs and heavily soiled vehicles are quoted on arrival. All prices include VAT.

Exterior hand wash from25 € Foam pre-soak, hand wash with the two-bucket method, wheel faces and arches, hand dried with microfibre. No brushes, no swirl marks.
Hand wash + spray wax from39 € Full exterior hand wash finished with a spray sealant for gloss and water beading. Good maintenance between bigger details.
Hand wax, paste wax from79 € Hand wash, chemical decontamination, then a proper paste wax applied and buffed by hand. Three to four months of protection.

Interior

Interior wash from35 € Thorough vacuum including boot, dashboard and trim wipe-down, door sills, interior glass. Seats and mats brushed out.
Interior deep clean from95 € Everything in the interior wash plus seat and carpet shampoo, steam on touch points, leather cleaned and conditioned where fitted.
Pet hair removal from30 € Add-on to any interior service. Rubber brushes and compressed air to lift embedded hair from seats, carpets and boot.

Packages

Wash inside & out from55 € Exterior hand wash and the full interior wash in one visit. Our most booked package, done in about an hour.
Full detailing from189 € Interior deep clean, exterior hand wash, decontamination, machine polish of light defects, paste wax, glass sealed, tyres dressed. Plan for half a day.
Engine bay cleaning from45 € Careful degrease and rinse with electrics protected, plastics dressed. Sensible before selling the car.
Headlight polishing from40 € Both headlights wet-sanded, polished and sealed. Restores cloudy lenses and improves night visibility.

How a visit works

No apps, no queues at a machine. Four steps and you drive out with a clean car.

  1. Call and book a time

    Ring +358 44 2444140 during opening hours. Tell us the car and what it needs, and we give you a time and an honest price on the phone.

  2. Drive in to Hannuksenpelto

    We are at Hannuksenpelto 2B in the Suomenoja business cluster, a couple of minutes from the Länsiväylä exit. Free parking right outside the door.

  3. Pick a package, confirm the price

    We walk around the car together, agree the package and the final price before any work starts. No surprises when you pay.

  4. Coffee while you wait

    A standard wash takes 30 to 60 minutes. Have a coffee in our waiting corner or take a walk to the Suomenoja shore. Full detailing is best left with us for the morning.
